Two decades of turning emerging technology into working infrastructure.

Regulated finance, technology, product, operations and entrepreneurship, in one career.

The path so far

  1. 1999

    Re-engineering at 17

    Works for a boutique accountancy firm and re-engineers its processes, moving all of them online.

  2. 2000

    The dot-com bubble pops

    Sees first-hand how quickly a market can turn.

  3. 2004

    JPMorgan

    Joins as a fund accountant. In 2005, told the processes he runs every day have always been the same and always will be, he teaches himself to code and automates much of his own role. Moves through business analysis and software development into project and product management, finishing as product manager for commodities back-office technology. Works through the 2008 financial crisis from the inside.

  4. 2013

    Blockchain

    Looks at how a new technology called blockchain could replace SWIFT messaging, and becomes interested in programmable, digitally native financial infrastructure.

  5. 2016

    4Com

    Product manager designing sales application technology, including a flagship app that drove significant new business.

  6. 2017

    Tokenisation

    Pitches the tokenisation of real-world assets, and is told it is a crazy idea that will never catch on.

  7. 2018

    JPMorgan, again

    Returns as Product Manager & Innovation Lead and leads a team that designs, builds and patents an AI meeting minute-taker. It helps automate stenography and supports people with auditory disabilities. Funding proves hard: the idea is good, investors say, but not a big enough problem to build a business around. The technology is the subject of a patent.

  8. 2019–20

    Avora

    Joins an AI analytics startup and progresses from head of onboarding to Head of Operations to COO, scaling the company from 12 to 60+ people. When COVID hits, restructures the company in the middle of a pivot.

  9. 2021

    YOP Finance

    COO. Takes over a struggling DeFi yield platform and grows it to $1M in total value locked within 60 days.

  10. 2022–24

    Founder years

    The crypto industry explodes, and one memorable morning is spent moving millions of dollars while Tether depegs. Co-founder and CEO of Finrock (tokenised real assets) and co-founder and COO of Powered by Ethos. CEO of Bento, a crypto wallet company, from 2023 to 2024.

  11. 2024

    Spark

    Head of Operations, first hire after the founders, launching an AI trading bot.

  12. Now

    Alludium

    Chief Commercial Officer, helping build and commercialise AI-native software designed around real professional workflows, rather than adding an assistant to existing software.

From dot-coms to DeFi, from banks to blockchains, one thing has stayed constant: disruption is the rule, not the exception. If you can survive the chaos, you might just shape what comes next.
