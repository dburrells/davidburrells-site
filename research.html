The framework

Three eras of AI

AI 1.0

Think

Models that know things and answer questions.

AI 2.0

Think and act

Assistants that use tools and carry out tasks on our behalf.

AI 3.0

Think, act and transact

Agents that hold identity, spend money and settle with one another. This is the agentic economy.

The questions behind the series

Once machines become economic participants, hard questions arrive quickly. Each paper takes on one of them.

  • How does an agent prove who it is?
  • What is it authorised to do?
  • Who owns what?
  • How does it transfer value?
  • How do we keep an immutable record of what happened?
  • How do millions of autonomous systems transact with one another?
