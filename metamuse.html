MetaMuse and the agentic economy

The thinking behind the work: a paper series, a weekly blog, and the venture I am building from them.

The venture

MetaMuse

When software agents start to buy and sell on our behalf, every transaction carries risk: a mistake, a dispute, a counterparty that does not deliver.

MetaMuse is my venture for that problem. Its first proposition, Agentic Capital Market, is transaction underwriting for machine commerce: pricing and standing behind the risk of what agents do, so that businesses and people can let them transact with confidence.

MetaMuse is separate from my work at Alludium.

Visit metamuse.co.uk → Join the waitlist

How the writing and the venture fit together

The papers are where I work out the ideas in public: what agents need in order to be trusted, how machine markets behave, and what goes wrong. MetaMuse is where I put the conclusions to work.

Paper Three, for example, argues that mistakes on machine trades should be handled as a priced feature of the contract, with undo windows and insurance built into the rails. That is the problem MetaMuse exists to solve.
